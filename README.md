## Projects

**[TidyLens](https://github.com/jhashw/tidylens)** · React, client-side JS
A browser-based tool for cleaning datasets and running a first pass of exploratory analysis. You declare what each column should contain, it enforces the rules, and you get a cleaned file plus linked charts. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, which matters because the datasets most worth cleaning are the ones you can't paste into a website. Built from a [written specification](https://github.com/jhashw/tidylens/blob/main/SPEC.md) that I revised as real data exposed gaps in it.
→ [Try it](https://jhashw.github.io/tidylens)

**[Food Delivery Operations Dashboard](https://github.com/jhashw/food-delivery-operations-dashboard)** · Power BI, DAX
Three-page dashboard covering executive KPIs, customer retention, and operational bottlenecks. Found that quarter-over-quarter retention sat near 21% with roughly a third of customers ordering only once, and traced a Q4 decline in SLA attainment to prep-time deterioration at a small group of restaurants.

**[Walmart Sales in Stormy Weather](https://github.com/jhashw/walmart-stormy-sales-sql)** · SQL
End-to-end SQL analysis of how rain and snow events shift product demand across 45 stores. Identified weather-sensitive items, separated snow-driven from rain-driven demand, and found anticipatory buying spikes the day before storms, with recommendations for pre-storm inventory positioning.

**[Uber Demand Forecasting](https://github.com/jhashw/uber-demand-forecasting)** · R
Hourly pickup demand forecasting for NYC boroughs, benchmarking exponential smoothing, harmonic regression, ARIMA and neural networks against naive baselines.

**[Life Expectancy Prediction](https://github.com/jhashw/life-expectancy-prediction)** · R
Predictive modeling across 179 countries using socio-economic and health indicators, comparing linear regression, regression trees, neural networks and XGBoost.
